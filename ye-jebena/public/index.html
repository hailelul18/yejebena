Our Story

A cup of coffee is an invitation.

In Ethiopia, coffee is never rushed. It is roasted, ground and brewed in front of you, poured from the jebena into small cups and shared in three rounds — abol, tona and baraka. It is how neighbours catch up, how guests are welcomed and how friendships are kept.

Ye’ Jebena (የጀበና) was created to bring that feeling to a modern, comfortable space: a place where quality coffee, handcrafted Ethiopian details and genuine hospitality come together.

  • HospitalityEveryone who walks in is a guest.
  • TraditionThe jebena at the heart of every cup.
  • QualityFresh coffee, prepared with care.
  • ConnectionA place to sit, talk and stay a while.
